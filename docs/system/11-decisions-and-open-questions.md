# 11. Decisions and open questions

> Draft for adviser review — flows and rules may change; see [README](README.md).

_Last updated: 23 October 2026._

This document records the decisions already made, mostly from SLU's internship orientation slides and the project team's discussions with faculty. **The open questions for the adviser are in one place: [Questions for our adviser](../ADVISER_QUESTIONS.md)**, numbered Q1–Q24. Other documents refer to them by number (for example "Q5").

**All decisions are provisional.** Every decision below, including those already built (for example D7's status-only checklist, the endorsement gate, the required hours and the hours rules), reflects the project manager's interpretation of SLU's internship instructions. If the adviser decides differently, the app and these documents will change.
