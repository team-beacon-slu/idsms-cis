# 11. Decisions and open questions

> Draft for adviser review — flows and rules may change; see [README](README.md).

_Last updated: 12 November 2026._

This document records the decisions already made, mostly from SLU's internship orientation slides and the project team's discussions with faculty. **The open questions for the adviser are in one place: [Questions for our adviser](../ADVISER_QUESTIONS.md)**, numbered Q1–Q24. Other documents refer to them by number (for example "Q5").

**All decisions are provisional.** Every decision below, including those already built (for example D7's status-only checklist, the endorsement gate, the required hours and the hours rules), reflects the project manager's interpretation of SLU's internship instructions. If the adviser decides differently, the app and these documents will change.

## Formerly noted for later (now done or decided)

- Monitoring log (supervisor checks, OJT visitations): **Done** (Q13): company page "Visits and supervisor check-ins" (visit on site, online, phone).
- Pull-out or mid-internship company transfer: **Decided** (Q22, option A): handled by staff by hand; not built.
- "University suspension" calendar type: **Done** (Q14, #188): days off belong to the host company (coordinator adds them on Calendar, with a reason); PH holidays only for companies in the Philippines.
- Withdrawing a submitted weekly report to draft: **Done** (Q21, option C, #187): "Take back to fix" until review and the Tuesday deadline.
- Flag for **habitual tardiness or absences** on the faculty dashboard: **Done** (#184): 3 or more in 4 weeks.
- Coordinator-posted department events on students' calendars: **Done** (#188): Calendar card, not a day off.
